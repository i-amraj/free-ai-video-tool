## Fliki kya hai?

AI video maker. Idea, script, blog post ya PowerPoint do → narrated video banata hai. AI voiceover (300 voices free plan me, 80+ languages, Hindi bhi), stock footage / AI images, captions, avatars, translation — sab ek jagah.

## Kaise kaam karta hai (2 minute)

1. [fliki.ai](https://fliki.ai) kholo, free signup karo — bina card ke
2. "New file" → apni ek line likho (jaise: "meri agency ki reel bana do")
3. Tool line ko scenes me todta hai; har scene par awaaz + tasveer khud bitha deta hai. Voice chuno (Hindi voices bhi hain)
4. Preview dekho → Export karo. Zero rupaye (free plan)

## Free plan me kya milega

- Price: **$0**, bina credit card ke, free forever
- 3 credits / month
- 300 standard voices, 80+ languages (Hindi bhi)
- Export: 720p, videos up to 5 minute (pricing page ke hisaab se)
- Basic AI image/video workflows

## Free plan me kya NAHI milega (imaandaari se)

- Har free video par **Fliki watermark** aata hai — chhota sa, par saaf dikhta hai
- **Commercial rights nahi** — free videos se kamaai nahi kar sakte
- 1080p, voice cloning, ultra-realistic voices — ye sab paid me hai
